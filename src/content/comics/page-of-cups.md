_The Page of Cups_ is the first installment of a supernatural mystery comic in two parts.

When a young fellow of the Occult Society takes up a new post as occult practician to a remote fishing village, the mistrust of the community, and the bizarre circumstances of his predecessor’s death, force him to question his vocation and the institution to which he’s devoted his life.

6″x9″, 56 black and white pages, saddle-stitched.
